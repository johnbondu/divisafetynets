FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need
to know before installation.

Have questions about safety nets, installation, materials or maintenance? Here are answers to the questions customers ask us most often.

Are safety nets suitable for balconies?

Yes. Safety nets are commonly installed on balconies to provide an additional layer of protection for children, pets and everyday household safety.

How long does installation take?

Installation time depends on the size, structure and type of space. Our team first checks the area and then recommends the appropriate installation method.

Are pigeon safety nets effective?

Properly installed pigeon safety nets can help restrict pigeons from entering balconies, ducts and other open areas while keeping the space usable.

Do you provide installation in Bangalore?

Yes. Divi Safety Nets provides professional safety net installation services across Bangalore and surrounding areas.

Can safety nets be installed for pets?

Yes. Pet safety nets can be installed on balconies and other suitable open spaces to create a safer environment for cats and dogs.

How do I get a quotation?

Contact our team by phone or WhatsApp. We can understand your requirement, check the installation area and guide you toward the right safety solution.
